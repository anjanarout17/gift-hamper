# Store Images

This folder is the simple image area for the catalogue.

## Hero images

- main-image.png — main large hero image. Recommended: portrait 4:5.
- left-image.png — small supporting hero image. Recommended: square 1:1.
- right-image.png — small supporting hero image. Recommended: square 1:1.

## Product images

- p1.png — Product 1
- p2.png — Product 2
- p3.png — Product 3
- p4.png — Product 4
- p5.png — Product 5
- p6.png — Product 6
- p7.png — Product 7
- p8.png — Product 8

Product images should ideally be square 1:1 with the full product clearly visible.

You can add more products as p9.png, p10.png, etc. When adding a new product, add its catalogue information in src/data/catalog.js.

## Naming rule

The filename describes the website position, not the type of product. A business can put any kind of gift/product in p1, p2, p3, etc.
